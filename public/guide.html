Getting Started & Navigation

MandaStrong Studio is a 23-page, fully browser-based filmmaking platform. There is nothing to install. Open it on any desktop, tablet, or mobile device in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.

How to move between pages

  • NEXT / BACK buttons — bottom of every page for sequential navigation.
  • Quick Access menu — always visible; jump directly to any of the 23 pages.
  • Number pad — pages are numbered 1–23 at the top of the navigation bar.

Saving your work

  • Hit SAVE PROJECT in the footer at any time to save your current state.
  • MY PROJECTS restores exactly where you left off — your clips, timeline, settings, and progress.
  • Generated clips are saved to your browser's IndexedDB storage for offline access.
Tip: Save your project before closing your browser tab. IndexedDB storage persists unless you clear site data.

Page 3 — AI Tools Hub (600+ Tools)

Browse and launch over 600 AI filmmaking tools organised into four categories: Writing, Image, Motion & VFX, and Enhancement.

How to use

  1. Select a category tab at the top (Writing / Image / Motion & VFX / Enhancement).
  2. Use the search bar to filter tools by keyword.
  3. Click any tool card to open its modal.
  4. Inside the modal: read the description, then hit AI CREATE to launch the tool, or UPLOAD to use your own file as input.
  5. Results save automatically to your Asset Manager (Page 11).

Tool categories include

  • Writing: loglines, treatments, scripts, episode arcs, character bibles, dialogue, scene rewrites, shot lists.
  • Image: AI image generation, style transfer, upscaling, background removal, stills-to-video.
  • Motion & VFX: motion graphics, transitions, green-screen, crowd simulation, slow motion, time-lapse.
  • Enhancement: upscaling, denoising, sharpening, frame interpolation, colour correction.

Page 6 — Voice Engine & Music Video Studio

Page 6 contains two major tools: the Voice Engine for narration and voiceover, and the Music Video Studio four-step wizard.

Voice Engine

54 voice characters covering a wide range of genders, ages, accents, and emotional styles.

Browsing voices

  • Use the filter buttons at the top to filter by gender, age group, and origin (British, American, Australian, Irish, etc.).
  • Each character card shows name, style tags, and accent.
  • Hit TEST on any card to hear a short preview of that voice reading a sample line.

Configuring a voice

  • PITCH slider — adjusts vocal pitch. Lower = deeper, higher = lighter.
  • RATE slider — controls speaking pace. Lower = slower and more deliberate.
  • PAUSE slider — adds natural pause time between sentences (in milliseconds).
  • VOLUME slider — overall output level.
  • MOOD selector — 14 moods including Calm, Dramatic, Excited, Sombre, Authoritative, Intimate, and more. Each mood adjusts pitch/rate/pause automatically.
James Settings (Documentary Narration): Click APPLY JAMES SETTINGS for the optimal documentary narration preset — Pitch 0.86, Rate 0.62, Pause 1600ms. James delivers a deep, measured British narrator voice ideal for wildlife, history, and social documentary.

Speaking your script

  1. Paste or type your script into the text area, or load a saved script from Page 5.
  2. Click PREPARE & SPEAK — this AI-formats your text for natural speech rhythm before speaking (adds natural pauses at sentence breaks, smooths ALL-CAPS, etc.).
  3. Click SPEAK NOW to play the formatted narration using the selected voice.
  4. Hit STOP to halt playback at any point.

Music Video Studio (also on Page 6)

A dedicated four-step wizard for creating complete music videos end-to-end.

  1. Step 1 — Track details: Enter title, artist name, genre, mood, tempo, and upload your audio file.
  2. Step 2 — Visual style: Choose video style (cinematic, lyric, performance, abstract), colour grade, effects pack, and edit style (fast cut, slow burn, narrative).
  3. Step 3 — Scene description: Describe your visual in detail — lighting, setting, characters, motion, colour palette.
  4. Step 4 — Generate: Hit GENERATE MUSIC VIDEO. Download the output and share directly to all platforms.

Page 7 — Image Tools

AI image generation and manipulation tools for creating visual reference, stills, posters, and production artwork.

Page 8 — Video Generator

The core clip generation engine. Describe any scene in natural language and the AI writes and renders a custom canvas animation for it. Every clip saves automatically to your Media Library.

Writing an effective prompt

Be as specific as possible. Include:

  • Setting and time of day (e.g. "rain-soaked London street at dusk")
  • Camera angle and movement (e.g. "slow low-angle push-in", "aerial crane shot")
  • Lighting and mood (e.g. "warm golden hour", "high-contrast noir")
  • Characters and action (e.g. "elderly man walks slowly, pauses at a window")
  • Colour palette (e.g. "desaturated teal and orange")
  • Visual effects (e.g. "dust particles in light beam", "rain on lens")

Using a reference image

  1. Click UPLOAD REFERENCE and choose an image.
  2. The generator will match the visual style, colour grade, and lighting of your reference.

Generating and saving

  1. Enter your scene description.
  2. Hit GENERATE CLIP.
  3. Preview the result in the viewer. Hit REGENERATE if you want a new variation.
  4. Click SAVE TO MEDIA LIBRARY — the clip is now available in the Asset Manager (Page 11) and the Timeline (Page 13).

Page 10 — Enhancement

Upscale, sharpen, denoise, and enhance any footage or image in your project.

  • AI Upscale: 2x or 4x resolution increase using AI super-resolution.
  • Denoising: Remove grain and digital noise while preserving detail.
  • Sharpening: Edge-aware sharpening for crisp detail.
  • Stabilisation: Remove camera shake from handheld footage.
  • Frame interpolation: Increase frame rate (e.g. 24fps to 60fps) for smoother playback.
  • Colour correction: One-click auto-balance for exposure, white balance, and contrast.

Select a clip from your Media Library, choose an enhancement, adjust the strength slider, and click ENHANCE. The processed clip is saved alongside the original.

Page 11 — Asset Manager

Your complete media library. Every clip generated on Page 8, every image created, every file you upload, and every enhanced asset lives here.

Uploading assets

  • UPLOAD FILES — select video, image, audio, PDF, or document files from your device.
  • Drag & drop — drag files directly onto the Asset Manager area.
  • Google Drive — connect your Drive account to pull files from the cloud.

Managing assets

  • Click any thumbnail to preview the asset in the viewer.
  • Hover over an asset and click the X to delete it.
  • Click the pencil icon to rename an asset.
  • Assets are sorted by date created by default; use the sort toggle to switch to name order.
Important: Assets are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Do not clear site data if you want to keep your library. For permanent storage, download your important clips via the download button on each asset card.

Page 12 — Storyboard

Plan your production visually, shot by shot, before committing to the timeline.

  • Each storyboard cell represents one shot or scene.
  • Assign an image or clip from your Asset Manager to each cell.
  • Add shot notes: camera angle, movement, dialogue cue, mood.
  • Drag cells to reorder your shot sequence.
  • Export the storyboard as a PDF for use in pre-production meetings.

Your storyboard order feeds directly into the Timeline on Page 13 — hit POPULATE TIMELINE FROM STORYBOARD to auto-arrange clips in the order you planned.

Page 13 — Timeline Editor

The professional multi-track editor. Arrange, sequence, and sync all elements of your film here.

Tracks

  • VIDEO track — your main visual clips in sequence.
  • VOICE track — narration and dialogue clips.
  • MUSIC track — background score and sound design.
  • EFFECTS track — sound effects, transitions, and VFX overlays.

Working with the timeline

  1. Drag clips from the Asset Manager panel (left side) onto the appropriate track.
  2. Clips snap to the playhead position.
  3. Drag the right edge of a clip to trim its out-point.
  4. Drag a clip left or right to reposition it in time.
  5. Click a clip to select it; press DELETE to remove it from the timeline (the asset stays in your library).

Auto-populate

Click SYNC ALL TRACKS to automatically populate all four tracks from your Media Library in chronological order. This is the fastest way to get a first assembly cut.

Film duration

Set your target film duration using the duration selector: 60 minutes, 90 minutes, or 180 minutes. The timeline ruler scales to match. Duration limits depend on your subscription plan.

Rendering

When your timeline is complete, hit RENDER to send it to the Render Engine on Page 16.

Page 14 — Colour Grade

Apply cinematic colour grades to your entire timeline or to individual clips.

Tools available

  • LUT presets — one-click cinematic looks: Teal & Orange, Bleach Bypass, Warm Vintage, Cold Blue, Noir, Vivid, Log-to-Rec709.
  • Tone controls — Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.
  • Colour controls — Temperature, Tint, Saturation, Vibrance.
  • HSL panel — adjust hue, saturation, and luminance for specific colour ranges.
  • Vignette — add a subtle edge darkening for cinematic focus.
  • Grain — add organic film grain texture.
Tip: Apply your grade to the whole timeline first for consistency, then use per-clip overrides for scenes that need individual treatment (e.g. flashbacks, dream sequences).

Page 15 — Audio Mixer

Balance your four audio tracks for a professional mix.

Track controls

  • MUSIC — background score level.
  • EFX — sound effects level.
  • MASTER — overall output volume.
Documentary mix (recommended): VOICE 85 · MUSIC 40 · EFX 50 · MASTER 85
Music video mix (recommended): MUSIC 75 · VOICE 60 · EFX 40 · MASTER 85

Saving presets

Enter a preset name and click SAVE PRESET to store your current mix settings. Load any saved preset from the dropdown for future projects.

Page 16 — Render Engine

Compile your completed timeline into a finished film file.

Render settings

  • Resolution: 1080p (Creator plan) · 4K (Pro plan) · 8K (Studio plan).
  • Codec: H.264 (wide compatibility) or VP9 (better quality at smaller file size).
  • Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic) · 25fps (PAL/broadcast) · 30fps (web/social).
  • Audio: AAC 320kbps stereo.

Render process

  1. Review your settings.
  2. The engine checks all timeline clips. Any missing or incomplete clips are flagged — click AUTO-REGENERATE MISSING to fill gaps automatically.
  3. Hit START RENDER.
  4. A progress bar shows render status. Do not close the tab during rendering.
  5. When complete, the output file appears in your Asset Manager and you are automatically taken to the Preview page (Page 17).

Page 17 — Preview

Full-screen preview of your rendered film before final export.

  • If anything needs adjustment, use the BACK TO EDITOR button to return to the Timeline without losing your render settings.
  • When satisfied, click APPROVE & EXPORT to proceed to Page 18.

Pro Tips & Troubleshooting

Pro Tips

  • Use PREPARE & SPEAK (not just SPEAK NOW) for narration — the AI formatting step dramatically improves natural speech rhythm.
  • Generate 3–4 variations of each scene on Page 8 and pick the best — regeneration costs nothing.
  • Apply your colour grade on Page 14 before rendering, not after — it bakes into the final file and saves processing time.
  • Use SYNC ALL TRACKS on the timeline for a rough cut first, then refine manually.
  • VP9 codec gives 20–30% better quality at the same file size as H.264 — use it for Vimeo and download; use H.264 for YouTube and TikTok.
  • Save your project before every render to avoid losing progress.

Troubleshooting

Video clip not generating
Try a shorter, more specific prompt. Avoid very abstract descriptions. Reload the page and try again if the generator hangs for more than 30 seconds.
Voice not speaking
Check that your browser allows audio playback. Chrome may block autoplay — click anywhere on the page first, then try SPEAK NOW again. Ensure the text field is not empty.
Asset not appearing in timeline
Go to Page 11 and confirm the clip is in your library. If it shows there, return to the timeline and use SYNC ALL TRACKS to re-populate. If still missing, try saving the project and reloading.
Upload failed
Check the file format is supported (MP4, MOV, AVI, MP3, WAV, JPG, PNG, PDF). Large files may take 10–30 seconds. Try compressing video files before upload if they are above 500MB.
Render taking too long
This is normal for long-form films. A 90-minute film may take 5–10 minutes to render at 4K. Keep the tab open and active. Do not switch to another browser tab repeatedly as this may throttle browser processing.
Google Drive not loading
Ensure you have granted the necessary Google permissions when prompted. Try using the regular file upload instead. Check your internet connection.
